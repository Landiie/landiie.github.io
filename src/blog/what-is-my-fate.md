It's been becoming harder and harder to keep it together when I see my bestest friends talk so beautifully about their relationships with others; Partners, former partners, extremely close friends, etc.

Every one of them has their special someone to them. Someone above everything else.

I... don't think I've ever been that to anyone.

Everyday, I hear it. How much joy they feel just mentioning their name. How much they feel like their life has so much purpose, making their significant others satisfied. The lengths they are willing to go, just to have a single moment with them of even the most mundane of experiences, like watching tv together in the same room. It doesn't matter, the experience is elevated by their mere presence. It could be anything, and it means the world to them.

Driving across states. Flying across country borders. All just to see them.

It's true love for one another. True passion. Mutual amongst each other. There is a clear bond between those folk. It feels so common, I feel surrounded by people who have something special amongst one another.

Then i look back at myself.

My friends matter more to me, then I will ever matter to them. It's always been this way. I don't keep my friends longer than a year a lot of the time because of how little I meant to them and how often I will go silent and it ends up being the last time I hear from them because they never cared enough to reach out, or, wanted to give me space but never bothered to check back after a period of time.

My former partners afaik are no longer on this plane minus my most recent ex, who, despite giving me an experience that resembled a beautiful bond, might've never been truthful with how our breakup went, where he ghosted me for almost a full year while I lived with constant anxiety attacks trying to get a hold of him and make sure he was alright.

Almost a **whole year** of that, out of our 4 years of being together, only for us to break up the moment I get to talk to him again, and be told some things that make me doubt anything we ever did together was genuine.

And I just have to wonder to myself...

...what is my fate?

I'm a very intimate, shy, private person. I valued each and every one of friends who i deemed close to me, and I wanted to do everything I could to make them happy, but, at the time, I felt very out of place knowing the kind of people they had in their lives. I felt like I was trying too hard for friends who didn't ever see me as anything more than just a mutual, and already had their fix with their more important relationships.

I was always so interested in everything they were doing, but nobody was interested in what I was doing. Nobody asked about me. Nobody was curious what I did, how I worked, what made me smile.

So, I isolated myself.

And only after a couple years, I decided to try to make friends again.

It's been really rocky, but, my current close friends I love to death, but I feel that voice in the back of my head pestering me again, as it did years prior.

- Do they think about me?
- Do I matter to them at least a little bit?
- Do they have interest in my life?

I sound so egotistical, That I want people to think about me; To acknowledge me beyond when I shove myself in their spaces.

But I see it all the time with friendships.

Why do I feel like i've never had that?

My current friends definitely show more interest in me than any other friend OR partner i've EVER had, but, how much of that is real?

I don't really understand friendship. I don't know what makes me become something that matters to someone more than just a simple acknowledgement. It's so stressful for me, I don't want to make mistakes. I don't want to overstep any boundaries. I don't want to assume I mean more to someone than I actually do. I love my friends so much that I feel safer not saying much of anything so they don't have to address me.

While I feel like this is the best way to keep a friendship, it also means I often become a fly on the wall which will slowly make me turn into someone that nobody is ever interested in.

But how can people so confidently assume they are ok to speak in any context? That they are welcomed to dm someone, to speak in a vc, to exist amongst their peers?

How can someone do that without fear that they are making a mistake exposing themself like that, making themselves the center of attention?

How do they do it and it always works out for them, and they have good conversations with folk?

How do they exist without fear of being rejected and thrown in a corner?

It's so frustrating, it's so heartbreaking.

I'm full of love and care for those who mean a lot to me, but, I never act upon any of it out of fear of scaring them away and permanently decreasing our bond.

I've read book after book, on how to be more approachable. How to speak with confidence that I'm wanted somewhere. How to love gently, without being overbearing for my friends.

I'm so robotic, so unnatural; But I don't understand how anyone was supposed to learn these, what seem like, basic interactions to them.

And yet I see them constantly talk about people who mean the world to them. Partners, close friends, etc. Just like my friends from years ago.

How do you become someone of that calibere? Is it even meant for someone like me?

Am I not supposed to be a person that is capable of experiencing something like that?

Then why do I have such love in my heart, why do I have such intimacy, such deep desire to satisfy those that mean so much to me, to do as much as I can to make them happy, to make them smile 24/7?

Why was I born like this? Am I broken?

Book after book, article after article, explaining how to be a good friend. How to properly care for those that mean a lot to you. Years ago they always failed, nothing I learned worked. Why would they work now with my new friends?

Why do I even have to learn this in the first place when it feels like everyone else was naturally born with a talent to know when they are wanted, when they are allowed to speak in a group, when they can comfortably be egotistical without coming off as such, and constantly be rewarded in the form of a strengthening bond with their friends?

These thoughts have plagued my mind ever since I went to elementary school. I was always the kid left out. The kid who nobody ever wanted to speak to. The kid who was awkward. The kid who tried too hard whenever someone showed an ounce of interest, thus, scaring them away.

It was those years that made me feel like I was truly cursed, and even today, I still firmly believe that I'm not somebody who can be loved, but, am forced to have so much love in my heart for those who show an ounce of interest in me, but, knowing my curse, I am too afraid to approach. too afraid to speak. too afraid to exist. too afraid to harm what little interest there is. only for it to whittle away into nothingness because I never acted upon it. I never rewarded their interest because I was too scared.

And my current friends, I'm scared they will face the same fate. I'm scared their interest doesn't get rewarded because im too scared to assume I mean anything, that what i think is their interest, is even interest in the first place.

I'm writing this because it's currently soon-to-be a convention where two of my three closest friends will be at. One of those friends is meeting their significant other for the first time at the convention, and everyday it's just been so much adoration for one another and constantly talking about all the things they will do together, so much love, so much intimacy, so much wonder, so much joy. It's even gotten my other friend to mention their really close friend, how much intimacy and joy they have with them too, how much of a bond they have, how much they trust one another.

It's so beautiful. It makes me so happy for them. It makes me feel overjoyed that they have that and can retreat to that in a time of need.

But it burns a hole in my cursed heart, knowing I can never get an experience like that.

Knowing I don't know how to be a good friend

Knowing I don't know how to progress a friendship. How to matter more to someone. How to make someone feel good. How to make them feel special. How to let them know how much they mean to me. How to make them feel wanted.

I'm too scared to assume I matter enough to even approach them unprompted, to strike up a friendly conversation.

I'm passive. I wait for interaction, when people have no reason to interact with me when all i do is sit in a corner and smile.

I never say what is on my mind, I only answer when spoken to.

It's a hell I can never escape.

And I have no idea how I got here.

I feel like my purpose on this planet is to instead just make software and tools for communities in need. People who will only ever know me for the work I do, and nothing more. A factory that churns out stepping stones that i place on my body.

Forever a bridge, forever cursed.

I'm trying so hard, for the current friends I have. but I also feel like im not trying at all. I feel like I'm letting my friendships die. friendships that mean everything to me. friendships i feel like i can never say how much they mean to me. friendships that literally make me feel like my life has changed, but I can't figure out how to maintain.

I love my friends to death, so much. But unfortunately, I'm me. Cursed.

Forever Landie.
